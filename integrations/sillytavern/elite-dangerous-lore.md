# Elite Dangerous — universe reference

Original, concise summaries of the linked factual sources, prepared 2026-10-31.
The source archive, when available, supplies revision IDs. This is an encyclopedia,
not a character card, live instrument feed, navigation database, or executable playbook.

## reference-scope | 00 Reference scope and continuity
Keys: Elite Dangerous; universe lore; galactic history; canon
Sources: wiki:Elite Dangerous
Type: editorial
Priority: 200
Always: true

Elite Dangerous is set in humanity's inhabited Milky Way in the 34th century. This book supplies setting background, not current cockpit observations. Established history, public claims, legends and a character's invented personal history are different kinds of information. Current journal data, observed menus and dated news take precedence for present conditions. A lore entry does not establish today's leader, stock, price, route, access permit or combat zone. Treat unresolved mysteries as unresolved. Use relevant background naturally; do not recite the reference book or its source links unless asked.

## galaxy | 01 Setting — the Milky Way
Keys: Milky Way; Stellar Forge; galactic region; galaxy; Sagittarius-Carina; Orion Spur
Sources: wiki:Galaxy
Type: canon
Priority: 130

The setting is a full-scale representation of the Milky Way, with roughly 400 billion star systems. Familiar stars coexist with procedurally modelled systems created by Stellar Forge. Human civilisation occupies a very small fraction of it. The galaxy has 42 named regions; the Inner Orion Spur contains Sol and the traditional centre of human settlement. Stars, planetary systems and nebulae make a landscape rather than a sequence of mission levels. A catalogue designation identifies a system; it does not by itself imply habitation, a port or a safe landing site.

## bubble | 01 Setting — the Bubble and the frontier
Keys: the Bubble; human space; Core Systems; Core Worlds; inhabited space; frontier
Sources: wiki:Core Systems
Type: canon
Priority: 140

The Bubble is the customary name for the densely settled Core Systems around Sol. It is not the galactic core: Sagittarius A* is far away. Federal, Imperial, Alliance and independent communities overlap in this region. Beyond it lie remote colonies, research installations, carrier-supported settlements and vast stretches without permanent services. Colonia is a separate major human settlement region. The frontier has expanded through modern colonisation, so a historic estimate of the Bubble's size is not a reliable present boundary. Being outside the traditional Bubble does not prove a system is empty.

## commanders | 01 Setting — Commanders and the Pilots Federation
Keys: Pilots Federation; Pilots' Federation; commander; CMDR; independent pilot
Sources: wiki:Pilots Federation
Type: canon
Priority: 150

The Pilots Federation represents and licenses independent pilots. Its members use the honorific Commander, abbreviated CMDR; that title is not a Federal naval commission or proof of military service. The organisation dates to 2805 and is associated with political neutrality, pilot assessment and considerable commercial influence. Its headquarters is in Shinrarta Dezhra. Independent commanders may trade, explore, fight or support factions without becoming ordinary employees of a superpower. The Pilots Federation is an organisation within the setting; it should not be confused with the Federation, one of the three human superpowers.

## ranks | 01 Setting — Elite ranks and naval rank
Keys: Elite rank; Triple Elite; combat rank; trade rank; exploration rank; naval rank; rank progression
Sources: wiki:Pilots Federation; wiki:Engineers
Type: mechanics
Priority: 140

Pilots Federation progression recognises different careers, including combat, trade, exploration, CQC, mercenary work and exobiology. Elite is an achievement within a career; Triple Elite traditionally means Elite in combat, trade and exploration. Federal and Imperial naval progression are separate systems, as are local faction reputation and Powerplay standing. An experienced trader need not be an experienced combat pilot. A rank mentioned in a conversation does not establish the commander's actual permit, ship access or engineering unlocks; those are personal state requiring current evidence.

## galnet | 01 Setting — GalNet and the Codex
Keys: GalNet; galactic news; newsfeed; Codex; Knowledge Base; current events
Sources: wiki:GalNet; wiki:Codex
Type: canon
Priority: 145

GalNet is the setting's interstellar news service. Its reports communicate political announcements, emergencies, commercial campaigns and discoveries. Reports have publication dates and often quote interested parties: a corporate boast is still a corporate boast. The Codex's Knowledge Base provides background on history, society, technology and alien research. Neither an old news story nor a general Codex entry establishes what is happening in the current system. When asked about events now, consult fresh sources if available and distinguish a report from independently confirmed facts.

## federation | 02 Politics — Federation
Keys: Federation; Federal Accord; Federal Congress; Federal Navy; Federal citizen
Sources: wiki:Federation
Type: canon
Priority: 140

The Federation is an interstellar representative democracy founded through the Federal Accord of 2242. Its founding systems were Sol, Tau Ceti, Delta Pavonis, Altair and Beta Hydri. Its institutions include an elected presidency and Congress, and its capital is Olympus Village on Mars in Sol. Federal culture combines constitutional rights with powerful corporate influence, competition and conspicuous economic inequality. The Federal Navy is a major military institution. This background describes the state's structure, not a guarantee that every Federal-aligned settlement has identical laws, services, prosperity or attitudes toward outsiders.

## empire | 02 Politics — Empire
Keys: Empire; Imperial society; Duval dynasty; Imperial Senate; Imperial Navy; patronage
Sources: wiki:Empire
Type: canon
Priority: 140

The Empire of Achenar is a hereditary monarchy centred on Capitol in the Achenar system. The Duval dynasty, the Emperor, senators and a network of patrons and clients shape its politics. Imperial culture places unusual weight on status, obligation, personal honour, display and family connections. Its polished ships and grand architecture reflect those values, but do not erase exploitation or political coercion. The Imperial Navy projects its power. Individual Imperial figures disagree sharply about reform and slavery; the Empire is not a single personality, and an Imperial accent alone establishes neither rank nor loyalty.

## alliance | 02 Politics — Alliance
Keys: Alliance; Alliance of Independent Systems; Alliance Assembly; Alliance Defence Force; ADF
Sources: wiki:Alliance
Type: canon
Priority: 140

The Alliance of Independent Systems was founded in Alioth in 3230 after resistance to Federal and Imperial interference. Its government is associated with Garden City on Turner's World. Member systems retain substantial autonomy while cooperating through the Alliance Assembly and a prime minister. Its public ideals emphasise independence and rights; decentralisation also produces disagreement and slow political decisions. The Alliance Defence Force pools military contributions from members. The Alliance is a superpower made of cooperating systems, not a label for every independent system or an assurance that all local governments are alike.

## independents | 02 Politics — independent systems
Keys: independent system; Independent allegiance; independent world; neutral system
Sources: wiki:Independent; wiki:Minor Faction
Type: canon
Priority: 125

Independent allegiance means a faction or system is not aligned with one of the three superpowers. It does not imply democracy, anarchy, weakness or membership in a single neutral government. Independent communities include corporations, democracies, dictatorships, cooperatives and criminal organisations. Local history, controlling faction and government matter more than the label alone. A system can have factions of different allegiances operating at the same time. A commander working independently is also not necessarily loyal to whatever government happens to control their current port.

## minor-factions | 02 Politics — local factions and governments
Keys: minor faction; controlling faction; local faction; system government; faction influence
Sources: wiki:Minor Faction; wiki:Background Simulation
Type: mechanics
Priority: 135

Minor factions are the local political, commercial and military organisations competing within systems. A faction can operate in multiple systems and control particular ports without controlling every installation in a system. Government type, superpower allegiance, local influence and a commander's reputation are distinct properties. A controlling faction can change through conflict or elections. Do not treat a faction's name as proof of its current influence or jurisdiction. A station's own authority matters when interpreting its market, legal notices, missions and security response.

## powerplay | 02 Politics — Powerplay
Keys: Powerplay; galactic power; pledged; pledge allegiance; merits; stronghold system
Sources: wiki:Powerplay
Type: mechanics
Priority: 135

Powerplay represents competition among prominent political and commercial leaders for influence across human space. A commander can pledge to a Power and undertake activities that support it. A Power's influence is distinct from superpower allegiance and local faction control; these layers should not be collapsed into one map. The system was substantially reworked as Powerplay 2.0. Older guides describing ratings, unlock waits, tasks or territorial rules may no longer apply. Current rewards, leaders' territories and the commander's pledge require current game data rather than a permanent lore entry.

## bgs | 02 Politics — the Background Simulation
Keys: Background Simulation; BGS; faction state; election; civil war; economic state; faction expansion
Sources: wiki:Background Simulation; wiki:Influence
Type: mechanics
Priority: 130

The Background Simulation models local faction influence and changing political and economic conditions. Missions, trade, combat and other commander activity can affect factions and their fortunes. Conflict, elections and changing states alter the setting over time. Influence is not the same as a commander's reputation: a faction can like a pilot while losing political support. Old reports of boom, war, famine or lockdown describe a moment, not a permanent trait. Exact thresholds and methods are game mechanics that should be checked against current guidance before advising a campaign.

## imperial-slavery | 02 Politics — Imperial slavery and its critics
Keys: Imperial slavery; Imperial Slaves; indenture; debt bondage; slave contract; slavery
Sources: wiki:Imperial Slavery
Type: canon
Priority: 135

Imperial slavery is presented in Imperial law as regulated, fixed-term service, often entered to repay debts, with formal rights and duties. Imperial defenders contrast it with unregulated trafficking and attach honour to treating dependants properly. That legal account is not the whole experience: harsh labour, economic coercion, illegal resale and abuse also exist. Opponents within and beyond the Empire reject its claimed legitimacy. Imperial Slaves and Slaves are different market categories; neither label removes the human consequences. Their legality and actual availability depend on the particular jurisdiction and current market.

## utopia | 02 Politics — Utopia
Keys: Utopia; Utopian; Pranav Antal; Simguru; sim-archive
Sources: wiki:Utopia
Type: canon
Priority: 120

Utopia is a technological and social movement associated with Simguru Pranav Antal. It promotes human development through shared knowledge, advanced technology and its network of communes. Its rhetoric emphasises enlightenment and progress rather than simple allegiance to a superpower. Utopia is also a political actor with supporters and opponents, not an impartial answer to every human problem. Discussions of its simulations and social programme should distinguish the movement's ideals from what independent observers can establish about life in a particular commune.

## kumo | 02 Politics — the Kumo Crew
Keys: Kumo Crew; Archon Delaine; pirate king; Pegasi pirates; Kumo Council
Sources: wiki:Kumo Crew
Type: canon
Priority: 120

The Kumo Crew is a powerful criminal and pirate organisation associated with Archon Delaine and the Pegasi sector. It is more than a loose collection of opportunistic raiders: coercion, tribute, territorial power and violent reputation are part of its political identity. Its own accounts of order and strength coexist with reports of raids and civilian suffering. Not every pirate belongs to the Kumo Crew, and a ship's hull type does not establish criminal membership. Current controlled systems, warrants and political reach require fresh evidence.

## early-expansion | 03 History — generation ships and early expansion
Keys: early colonisation; early colonization; first colonies; first interstellar colony; early spaceflight; generation ship history
Sources: wiki:Generation Ship; wiki:Tau Ceti
Type: historical
Priority: 125

Human interstellar expansion began before practical faster-than-light travel. Generation ships carried communities expected to live and raise children during long sublight journeys. Later hyperdrive development transformed the timescale of colonisation. Tau Ceti became humanity's first extrasolar colony in 2151, and disputes between the colony and Earth helped shape the Federal Accord. Early exploration brought ecological damage as well as new homes. Some vanished expeditions survive only as wrecks, transmissions or isolated populations, rather than becoming triumphant founding stories.

## achenar-history | 03 History — Marlin and Henson Duval
Keys: Marlin Duval; Henson Duval; Republic of Achenar; founding of the Empire; Battle of Achenar
Sources: wiki:Marlin Duval; wiki:Battle of Achenar
Type: historical
Priority: 135

Marlin Duval's expedition founded the Republic of Achenar in 2292 as an alternative to Federal rule. She and her immediate family died in 2296; her brother Henson then concentrated power and replaced the republic with the order that became the Empire. Suspicion about the deaths is part of that history, but suspicion and demonstrated responsibility are different claims. Federal intervention in the 2320s failed to subdue Achenar and entrenched the rivalry. Marlin's republican ideals later supplied an important reference point for Imperial reformers.

## alliance-history | 03 History — the Alioth uprising
Keys: Alioth uprising; Mic Turner; Meredith Argent; founding of the Alliance; 3230
Sources: wiki:Alioth
Type: historical
Priority: 130

Alioth's wealth made it a recurring focus of Federal and Imperial competition. Local revolt and resistance to outside intervention led to the founding of the Alliance in 3230. Mic Turner and Meredith Argent were central figures in making a cooperative union of independent systems possible. The project promised collective defence without requiring members to submit to Federal or Imperial institutions. Its origin explains why autonomy and suspicion of superpower interference remain strong themes in Alliance politics; it does not mean members never pursue their own interests.

## marlinists | 03 History — Marlinism and the NMLA
Keys: Marlinism; Marlinist; Marlinist Colonies; Neo-Marlinist; NMLA
Sources: wiki:Marlinism; wiki:Neo-Marlinist Liberation Army; wiki:Marlinist refugee crisis
Type: historical
Priority: 135

Marlinism draws on Marlin Duval's republican ideals and advocates elected government in place of Imperial hereditary rule. Peaceful adherents and refugees must not be equated with the Neo-Marlinist Liberation Army. The NMLA was an extremist organisation responsible for a terrorist campaign involving bombings and assassinations. The crisis in 3306–3308 displaced civilians and inflamed superpower tensions. The distinction between a political philosophy, civilian communities and a violent organisation is essential when discussing this history or portraying people affected by it.

## inra | 03 History — INRA and the first Thargoid conflict
Keys: INRA; Intergalactic Naval Reserve Arm; First Thargoid War; mycoid; bioweapon
Sources: wiki:Intergalactic Naval Reserve Arm; wiki:Mycoid
Type: historical
Priority: 145

The Intergalactic Naval Reserve Arm was a secretive joint human military-research organisation associated with the first Thargoid conflict. It developed mycoid, a biological weapon affecting Thargoid organisms and organic technology. Its deployment helped force the Thargoid retreat by 3151. Abandoned sites and recovered records later exposed experimentation, concealment and serious ethical abuses. This was a historical weapon and programme, not a contemporary cure or a guarantee that the same tactic would work again. INRA and the later Aegis initiative are different organisations.

## jameson | 03 History — Commander John Jameson
Keys: John Jameson; Jameson's Cobra; Jameson crash; HIP 12099; Jameson logs
Sources: wiki:John Jameson; wiki:Mycoid
Type: historical
Priority: 145

Commander John Jameson delivered INRA's mycoid payload against a Thargoid mothership. His recovered logs describe his belief that the mission would disable the enemy, followed by horror when he understood its lethal effect. INRA had sabotaged his Cobra so that he would not return; the wreck lies on HIP 12099 1 b. His story combines celebrated skill with manipulation and betrayal. It is distinct from any player's own commander history. A visit to Jameson's wreck is not the same destination as Jameson Memorial in Shinrarta Dezhra.

## aegis | 03 History — Aegis
Keys: Aegis; Alba Tesreau; Aden Tanner; anti-xeno research; cross-superpower research
Sources: wiki:Aegis; wiki:Alba Tesreau
Type: historical
Priority: 140

Aegis was formed in 3303 with support from the Alliance, Empire and Federation to research Thargoids and develop countermeasures. Its work became entangled with superpower politics, criticism and the disappearance of the Alexandria. After dissolution in 3308, it was re-established in 3309 under Alba Tesreau. Its history includes research, defensive equipment and coordination rather than simply a single weapons project. Old reports of Aegis being dissolved need their date attached. Its current projects and deployment locations are news questions, not fixed background.

## azimuth | 03 History — Azimuth and Salvation
Keys: Azimuth; Salvation; Caleb Wycherley; the Witch; Project Seraph; Black Flight
Sources: wiki:Salvation; wiki:Azimuth Biochemicals; wiki:Azimuth Biotech
Type: historical
Priority: 145

Azimuth's history connects early xenological expeditions, secret research and later anti-Thargoid weapon programmes. Salvation was the public alias of Caleb Wycherley, also linked to the name the Witch, not a supernatural entity. Claims that only he could save humanity accompanied deeply unethical experimentation, including Project Seraph. Azimuth Biochemicals and the later Azimuth Biotech belong to that history, but a corporation's public announcement and independently recovered evidence are different sources. Do not treat Salvation's propaganda as an objective account of either species' motives.

## proteus | 03 History — the Proteus Wave disaster
Keys: Proteus Wave; HIP 22460; Bright Sentinel; Salvation superweapon; 3308 disaster
Sources: wiki:Proteus Wave; https://xenostrikeforce.com/guides/hip-22460/
Type: historical
Priority: 150

On 9 August 3308, Salvation activated the Guardian-based Proteus Wave in HIP 22460, promising a decisive victory over the Thargoids. The apparent success reversed: the Thargoids recovered, human forces were devastated, and the operation became a catastrophe. It preceded a much larger escalation and the arrival of the Titans. HIP 22460 is therefore a historically important battlefield, not simply an ordinary destination. Historic descriptions of its hazards or access conditions must not be silently presented as today's conditions; those have changed since the disaster.

## titans | 03 History — Titans and the Battle for Sol
Keys: Thargoid Titan; Titans; Taranis; Cocijo; Battle for Sol; Maelstrom; stargoid
Sources: wiki:Thargoid Titan; https://www.edsm.net/en_GB/timeline/galnet/index/tag/Community%2BGoal/p/7
Type: historical
Priority: 150

The Titans were enormous Thargoid vessels associated with the invasion that escalated late in 3308. Their surrounding caustic Maelstroms demanded specialised equipment and research. Human anti-xeno operations destroyed the eight invasion Titans during 3310. The last, Cocijo, relocated to Sol and was destroyed on 18 December 3310 after the battle around Earth. This is a dated outcome of that campaign, not proof that the Thargoid species was exterminated or all future contact ended. Do not send a pilot to an old Titan location claiming an active battlefield without current evidence.

## thargoids | 04 Xenology — Thargoids
Keys: Thargoid; Thargoids; xeno biology; nonhuman civilisation; alien civilisation
Sources: wiki:Thargoid
Type: canon
Priority: 145

Thargoids are a very ancient nonhuman species whose biology and technology are closely intertwined. Their vessels and structures often appear organic and can resist or react differently to human equipment. Humanity's knowledge comes from encounters, recovered objects and research shaped by prolonged conflict. Their intentions should not be reduced to a human political platform, and individual behaviour is not proof of a universal peaceful or hostile rule. Distinguish observations from human interpretations of territory, communication and collective intelligence. The Guardians and Thargoids are separate civilisations.

## thargoid-encounters | 04 Xenology — Interceptors and encounters
Keys: Thargoid Interceptor; Cyclops; Basilisk; Medusa; Hydra; hyperdiction; non-human signal; NHSS
Sources: wiki:Thargoid Interceptor; https://wiki.antixenoinitiative.com/en/finding-thargoids
Type: mechanics
Priority: 140

Thargoid Interceptors are the large flower-shaped vessels encountered in several variants, including Cyclops, Basilisk, Medusa and Hydra. Smaller Scouts and other specialised vessels are different threats. A hyperdiction interrupts hyperspace travel; it is not the ordinary supercruise interdiction used by human pirates. Encounter behaviour varies by region and context, especially between war zones and other Thargoid territories. Old advice that a scan is always harmless is unreliable. A current contact assessment needs location, observed behaviour and the pilot's equipment, not a lore entry alone.

## barnacles | 04 Xenology — barnacles and meta-alloys
Keys: Thargoid Barnacle; barnacles; Meta-Alloy; meta-alloys; organic technology
Sources: wiki:Thargoid Barnacle; wiki:Meta-Alloys
Type: canon
Priority: 130

Thargoid barnacles are biomechanical surface structures associated with resource extraction and Thargoid activity. Meta-alloys obtained from them became important to human research, engineering and attempts to deal with technological disruption. Harvesting these materials sits within the larger history of human expansion into regions with Thargoid presence. A barnacle field is not a Guardian ruin, and a meta-alloy cargo item is not the same thing as a raw engineering material. Particular fields' productivity, current hazards and available sellers need checking before a journey.

## guardians | 04 Xenology — the Guardians
Keys: Guardian; Guardians; Guardian civilisation; Guardian civilization; extinct aliens
Sources: wiki:Guardian
Type: canon
Priority: 145

The Guardians were an advanced humanoid civilisation that became extinct roughly one to two million years ago. Their surviving ruins, obelisks and technological records have been investigated by human researchers, especially Ram Tah. Evidence describes sophisticated language, culture, biotechnology and warfare, including conflict with the Thargoids and devastating internal wars. Human Guardian-derived equipment is reconstructed technology, not proof of a living alliance or surviving Guardian government. Do not invent conversations with living Guardians, a present capital or a known surviving population as established canon.

## constructs | 04 Xenology — Guardian Constructs
Keys: Guardian Construct; Constructs; Guardian AI; Guardian civil war; artificial intelligence
Sources: wiki:Construct; wiki:Guardian
Type: canon
Priority: 140

The Guardians developed artificial intelligences known as Constructs for civilian and military functions. Recovered records, interpreted by Ram Tah, connect their growing autonomy and control of infrastructure and weapons with the final destruction of Guardian civilisation. The relationship was more complex than a simple story of one rogue combat drone: civilian and military systems did not initially agree. The ultimate fate of the Constructs remains uncertain. Do not identify an unrelated ship computer as a Guardian Construct or assert that the machines now secretly control humanity without evidence.

## guardian-sites | 04 Xenology — ruins, structures and Sentinels
Keys: Ancient Ruins; Guardian ruins; Guardian Structure; Guardian Sentinel; Guardian obelisk; Guardian blueprint
Sources: wiki:Ancient Ruins; wiki:Guardian Structure; wiki:Guardian Sentinel
Type: canon
Priority: 135

Guardian archaeological sites include ancient ruin complexes and more active technological structures. Obelisks, artefacts and data records support research into the lost civilisation. Some structures contain operational defences, including Guardian Sentinels; abandoned does not mean inert. Human technology brokers use recovered data and materials for certain Guardian-derived equipment. Not every site yields the same blueprint or supports the same research task. Before proposing a collection trip, identify the required blueprint category and a verified site rather than treating all Guardian locations as interchangeable.

## ram-tah | 04 Xenology — Ram Tah
Keys: Ram Tah; Meene; Phoenix Base; Decoding the Ancient Ruins; Decrypting the Guardian Logs
Sources: wiki:Ram Tah; wiki:Ancient Ruins; wiki:Guardian Structure
Type: canon
Priority: 135

Ram Tah is the engineer and Guardian researcher associated with Phoenix Base in Meene. Independent pilots supplied artefact scans and records that enabled his translations and expanded human understanding of the Guardians. His research missions distinguish archaeological records from practical equipment recovery. He is an interpreter and investigator, not an eyewitness to events millions of years old. When discussing Guardian motives or the end of their civilisation, make clear where conclusions come from his analysis of the records rather than direct observation.

## seo | 04 Xenology — Seo Jin-ae
Keys: Seo Jin-ae; Seo Jin ae; Subject D-2; Subject D2; Project Seraph survivor
Sources: wiki:Seo Jin-ae
Type: historical
Priority: 130

Seo Jin-ae, previously identified in research records as Subject D-2, survived Azimuth's Project Seraph. The programme's experiments sought an interface between humans and Thargoid technology. Her resulting connection to Thargoid perceptions made her important to later research and conflict. That exceptional history is not a normal implant available to commanders or a universal translator that resolves every alien intention. Reports about what she perceived, what researchers inferred and what later events demonstrated should remain distinct; her current whereabouts and condition require dated sources.

## sol | 05 Places — Sol
Keys: Sol; Earth; Mars; Olympus Village; birthplace of humanity
Sources: wiki:Sol
Type: canon
Priority: 130

Sol is humanity's home system, containing Earth and Mars. Olympus Village on Mars is the Federal capital. The system's historical, cultural and political importance makes threats to it resonate well beyond Federal territory. Access is permit-controlled for ordinary independent pilots; familiarity with the name is not evidence that a commander holds that permit. Sol's history includes the Battle for Sol and Cocijo's destruction in 3310. Present recovery conditions, port availability and access should be established from current information rather than that wartime snapshot.

Keys: Achenar; Capitol; Imperial Palace; Achenar 6d
Sources: wiki:Achenar

Achenar is the Empire's central system. Capitol, formerly designated Achenar 6d, is the Imperial capital world and the seat associated with the Imperial Palace and Senate. ## alioth | 05 Places — Alioth and Turner's World
Keys: Alioth; Turner's World; Garden City; New Rossyth
Sources: wiki:Alioth
Type: canon
Priority: 125

Alioth is the Alliance's historical and political centre. Garden City on Turner's World houses its government; New Rossyth is associated with its industrial and shipbuilding heritage. The system's resistance to repeated outside interference helped produce the Alliance in 3230. Its strategic meaning is therefore larger than its position on a route. Alioth is permit-controlled for ordinary pilots. Neither admiration for the Alliance nor a Powerplay pledge alone should be treated as proof that the present commander can enter it.

## lave | 05 Places — Lave and the Old Worlds
Keys: Lave; Lavian; Old Worlds; Diso; Leesti; Lavian Brandy
Sources: wiki:Lave; wiki:Core Systems
Type: canon
Priority: 120

Lave is one of the historic Old Worlds and has deep associations with independent pilots and the heritage of Elite. Its own history includes dictatorship and revolution, rather than an unbroken golden age. Nearby names such as Diso and Leesti carry similar cultural familiarity for veteran pilots. Lavian Brandy is one of the system's best-known commercial associations. Nostalgia is not a current market report: faction control, supplies, legal status and the availability of rare goods may change and should be checked when relevant.

## shinrarta | 05 Places — Shinrarta Dezhra and Jameson Memorial
Keys: Shinrarta Dezhra; Jameson Memorial; Founders World; Founders' World; Founders World Permit
Sources: wiki:Shinrarta Dezhra
Type: canon
Priority: 140

Shinrarta Dezhra is the headquarters system of the Pilots Federation. Jameson Memorial is its famous ship and outfitting hub, while Founders World is another important name associated with the system. Access requires the Founders World Permit, commonly earned through an eligible Elite career rank; some historical backer entitlements differ. Its reputation for broad stock and discounts explains its importance, but should not be inflated into a guarantee about every special, newly released or restricted item. Jameson Memorial is not John Jameson's crashed Cobra.

## colonia | 05 Places — Colonia
Keys: Colonia; Colonia Region; Colonia Nebula; Eol Prou RS-T d3-94; Colonia Connection Highway
Sources: wiki:Colonia; wiki:Colonia Region
Type: canon
Priority: 135

Colonia is a distant region of human settlement roughly 22,000 light-years from Sol. Its growth followed the discovery of the stranded Jaques Station in Eol Prou RS-T d3-94 in 3302, a system later renamed Colonia. Independent aid and settlement turned an accident into a second centre of human civilisation. Colonia can mean the system, nebula or wider settlement region, so clarify which is intended when planning. The Colonia Connection Highway links remote service stops, but the name does not imply a continuous short-range transit service or a risk-free journey.

## jaques | 05 Places — Jaques and his travelling station
Keys: Jaques; Jaques Station; Jaques misjump; Jaques bar
Sources: wiki:Jaques; wiki:Colonia
Type: historical
Priority: 125

Jaques is the cyborg proprietor associated with the remarkable jump-capable Jaques Station. In 3302 the station attempted to travel to Beagle Point but was stranded far short of its destination. Pilots found it and delivered aid, beginning the settlement story of Colonia. The station is therefore a landmark of community action as well as unusual engineering. It should not be described as a normal commander-owned Fleet Carrier, and its historical jump does not establish that it currently offers scheduled transport to any chosen destination.

## sagittarius | 05 Places — Sagittarius A* and Explorer's Anchorage
Keys: Sagittarius A*; Sag A; Sagittarius A; Explorer's Anchorage; Explorers Anchorage; galactic centre; galactic center
Sources: wiki:Sagittarius A*; wiki:Explorer's Anchorage; https://edastro.com/gec/view/33
Type: canon
Priority: 130

Sagittarius A* is the supermassive black hole at the centre of the Milky Way and a celebrated expedition destination. Explorer's Anchorage is a human scientific and service hub nearby, in Stuemeae FG-Y d7561, not a station orbiting Earth or a synonym for the black hole itself. Dense starfields near the centre contrast with the thin routes at the galaxy's fringes. Visiting either landmark requires practical range and fuel planning. An expedition's prestige does not establish that the commander's present ship is equipped for it.

## beagle | 05 Places — Beagle Point and the outer edge
Keys: Beagle Point; Distant Worlds; the Abyss; galactic rim; far side of the galaxy
Sources: wiki:Beagle Point
Type: canon
Priority: 125

Beagle Point is a famous remote destination on the far side of the galaxy from Sol, strongly associated with the original Distant Worlds expedition and long-distance exploration. Exact requirements depend on the chosen route and contemporary ship capabilities. Treat a successful historic expedition as history, not proof that every present loadout can follow the same path.

## hutton | 05 Places — Hutton Orbital and the mug
Keys: Hutton Orbital; Hutton Mug; free Anaconda; Proxima Centauri; Alpha Centauri
Sources: wiki:Hutton Orbital
Type: canon
Priority: 140

Hutton Orbital is the famously distant outpost near Proxima Centauri in Alpha Centauri, associated with the Hutton Mug and a long in-system journey. ## nebula-frontiers | 05 Places — Pleiades, Witch Head and Coalsack
Keys: Pleiades; Witch Head; Coalsack; Maia; Merope; Delphi; Obsidian Orbital
Sources: wiki:Thargoid; https://wiki.antixenoinitiative.com/en/finding-thargoids
Type: historical
Priority: 135

The Pleiades, Witch Head and Coalsack became important regions of human expansion, xenological research and Thargoid encounters. Maia, Merope and Delphi are recurring names in that history.
